Charge Ready Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Charge Ready is a casual game about running an electric car charging station. This page explains what data the game uses and why. We keep it short because the game collects very little.

What stays on your device

Your progress, cash and settings are saved on your own device so the game can continue where you left off. We do not receive this save data. Deleting the app or clearing its storage removes it.

Ads

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. Some ads are optional videos you choose to watch for a reward, and some appear between stages. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and how you interact with ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change that choice at any time. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

Feedback and play statistics

If you send feedback from the game, we receive your rating, your comment and basic context such as game version, station, screen size and device model. During testing the game may also record anonymous play statistics like session length and stage progress. None of this includes your name, email or contacts.

Children

Charge Ready is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent to santepeli@gmail.com.
